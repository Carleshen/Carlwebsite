HOW I WORK

A clear route from brief to delivery.

Structured enough to keep work moving; flexible enough to fit the content, people and context.

  1. 01

    Analyse

    Clarify the audience, need, source material and delivery context.

  2. 02

    Design

    Shape objectives, sequence, assessment and a practical learning approach.

  3. 03

    Develop

    Build the course and supporting materials, using the right tools for the format.

  4. 04

    Review

    Check content, interaction, usability and alignment with the brief.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    Prepare the agreed learning assets for handover or learning-platform use.

AI-assisted research, drafting and QA can support the workflow; design decisions stay grounded in the learning need and subject-matter review.

A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Experience across the whole learning build.

I’m Carl, an instructional designer and learning content developer with approximately 11 years of experience. My work spans eLearning, instructor-led learning, video and the practical materials that help people use what they learn.

I’m comfortable working with subject matter experts and complex source material, taking a project from analysis and design through development and QA. My experience includes technology, financial services, compliance, project management, sales, health and safety, and professional learning.

I bring established instructional design practice to each project while making thoughtful use of AI-assisted tools and automation in research, production and review workflows. The aim is useful, well-considered learning, with technology supporting the work rather than driving it.
